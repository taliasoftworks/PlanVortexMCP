# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is doing their best with the information they had.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, in pull requests and in reviews.
- Criticise the code, never the person who wrote it.
- Accept that a maintainer's time is limited, and that "not now" is a legitimate answer.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing somebody else's private
information, and any conduct that would reasonably make somebody feel unwelcome.

## Reporting

Write to <contact@planvortex.com>. Reports are handled privately, and the person who reports is
never identified without their agreement.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to anybody
representing the project in public.
